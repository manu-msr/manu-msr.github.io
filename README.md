## About

<img src="https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/15841718?v=4" align="right" width="30%" height="68%" vspace=22 hspace=10>

I am a computer scientist with a Master’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering (2023) from the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), completed at the Institute for Research in Applied Mathematics and Systems (IIMAS). I previously earned my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science (2019) from UNAM’s Faculty of Science.

My research interests focus on three main areas: Theory of Computation, artificial intelligence, and data science. In Theory of Computation, I am interested in the formal foundations of computer science, including computability, formal languages, semantics, and the mathematical structure of programming languages. In artificial intelligence, my work includes the study and evaluation of computational systems, with particular attention to their sociotechnical dimensions and to the ways in which algorithms interact with social contexts. In data science, I am interested in the analysis, management, and interpretation of data, as well as in computational approaches for extracting knowledge and developing data-driven solutions. Across these three areas, I also maintain a complementary interest in computer science education, particularly in studying how these topics are taught and learned and in developing learning and assessment instruments for computing education.

Since 2026, I have been a Full-Time Research and Teaching Faculty Member at the Autonomous University of Mexico City (UACM), within the College of Science and Technology. I also teach at UNAM’s Faculty of Science.
